# Candidates — Make the Judge Smile

Fourteen candidate lines were drafted, counted exactly by script, and duelled pairwise on the judge's criteria in order; the round-1 winner then defended its place against four fresh challengers in a revision round.

**Final winner (153 chars):** My son, five, left his last grape on my plate at lunch and said, 'You look like you need the sweet one today.' He was right about the day, and the grape.

**Runner-up (134 chars):** This morning I found a note in my lunchbox: 'Day 4,112 of choosing you.' Eleven years married — my wife's math is off, her aim is not.
